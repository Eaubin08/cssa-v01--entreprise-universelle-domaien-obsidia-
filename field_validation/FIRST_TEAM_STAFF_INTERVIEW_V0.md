# F3E — FIRST-TEAM STAFF FIELD VALIDATION V0

Status: OPTIONAL REAL-WORLD VALIDATION / NOT REQUIRED FOR SIMULATION

## Why this contact is useful

A current first-team physical-performance staff member can help validate the operational boundary around the first team without needing access to the whole club administration.

The most useful evidence is workflow evidence, not personal payroll data.

## Preferred questions

Ask only what the person is comfortable sharing:

- What legal entity appears as employer/contracting party?
- Is the role salaried, fixed-term, permanent, contractor or another arrangement?
- Who gives the operational brief and who validates work?
- Who changes the weekly schedule?
- How are training/match travel plans communicated?
- Who books or validates transport, hotel, meals and equipment?
- How are expenses submitted and approved?
- Is mileage reimbursed, and through what kind of process?
- What documents/forms are repeatedly required?
- Which tools/channels are actually used: email, WhatsApp, shared drive, planning app, paper, federation tools?
- What changes at the last minute most often?
- Which task still depends on one person remembering something?
- What proof remains after an expense or trip is validated?
- What is different between first-team staff and Association/youth staff?
- Who is the administrative contact when a staff member has a contract/payroll/expense problem?

## Particularly useful artefacts

Prefer de-identified or reconstructed examples:

- blank expense form;
- blank travel reimbursement form;
- blank staff onboarding checklist;
- generic weekly planning screenshot with names hidden;
- redacted travel instruction;
- redacted internal process note;
- contract TYPE and employing ENTITY only;
- a hand-written explanation of the expense/payroll workflow.

## Do not request player health/performance data

Do not collect:

- injury details;
- medical results;
- GPS/player load data tied to an identifiable player;
- rehabilitation/health records;
- private disciplinary information.

Those are not needed to validate the Administration V0.
