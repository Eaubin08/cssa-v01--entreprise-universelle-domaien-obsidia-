# 00 — INTENT

## Primary objective

Use a real organization — CSSA — to test and advance Obsidia V0.1 as an adaptable enterprise system without rebuilding the Core.

## Three-layer target

```text
UNIVERSAL / UDIP
        ↓
ADMINISTRATION
        ↓
CSSA
```

CSSA is not the universal model.
Administration is not the Core.
Universal is not a business ontology.

## Success question

Can Obsidia absorb a real organization and its administrative reality while preserving:
- KX108_ONLY;
- domain/world-agnostic kernel;
- explicit translations;
- proof/receipt surfaces;
- human validation where required;
- strict separation of domain meaning from authority?

## Secondary objective

Use CSSA as a real-world V0.1 reference implementation and extract only validated reusable structures upward.

## Out of scope for pre-forge

- no autonomous administrative agent;
- no kernel mutation;
- no irreversible external action;
- no claim of production readiness;
- no assumption that repeated human practice is automatically correct;
- no mixing of the separate CSSA Memory & Identity project into Administration.
