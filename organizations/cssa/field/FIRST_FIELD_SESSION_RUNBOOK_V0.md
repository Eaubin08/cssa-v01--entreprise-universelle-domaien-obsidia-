# FIRST CSSA FIELD SESSION — RUNBOOK V0

Goal: leave the first real session with enough evidence to build F3C without asking CSSA to redesign its work.

## 1. Pick one real recent match

Follow it from the first administrative trigger to closure.

Capture only:
- what arrived;
- where it arrived;
- who noticed it;
- who owned the next step;
- deadline;
- tool/channel;
- who could validate;
- what was finally done;
- what proof remained.

## 2. Pick one exception

Prefer one of:
- late fixture change;
- FMI unavailable;
- missing document/licence;
- referee/official change;
- commission decision;
- last-minute match-day change.

Record the difference between the normal path and what actually happened.

## 3. Compare public rule vs local practice

Use:
`organizations/cssa/field/templates/PUBLIC_RULE_VS_LOCAL_PRACTICE_V0.yaml`

Do not mark local practice as a rule just because staff say 'we always do it like that'.

## 4. Capture roles, not people first

Examples:
- ADMINISTRATION;
- SECRETARIAT;
- MATCHDAY_ORGANIZATION;
- PRESIDENT/VALIDATOR;
- TREASURY;
- EXTERNAL_LGEF_AUTHORITY.

Names can be added later only when operationally necessary.

## 5. Minimum target for first session

- 3 workflow families;
- 1 normal path;
- 1 exception path;
- 1 case with missing/ambiguous information;
- 1 real approval chain;
- 1 public-rule vs local-practice comparison;
- 1 repeated task dependent on human memory.

## 6. Do not connect write access

No send, no Footclubs write, no FMI submit, no official declaration.

First field goal is observation + structure + readonly classification.

## 7. After the session

Normalize each captured case with:
`FIELD_CASE_CAPTURE_V0.yaml`

Then classify each source:
`OBSERVED_DIRECTLY / STAFF_STATEMENT / INTERNAL_DOCUMENT / SYSTEM_SCREEN_STATE / EMAIL_THREAD / OFFICIAL_EXTERNAL_DECISION / LOCAL_CHECKLIST / INFERRED_PATTERN`.

Only CSSA-validated clean cases may later reach an ALLOW for bounded internal READONLY analysis.
